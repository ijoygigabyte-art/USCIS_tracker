🍪 How to Get Your myUSCIS Session Cookie
1

Log in to myUSCIS

Open my.uscis.gov in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox and sign in with your email, password, and two-factor code (2FA) until you see your account dashboard.

2

Open Browser Developer Tools

On that same logged-in myUSCIS tab, press F12 (or Ctrl + Shift + I on Windows, Cmd + Option + I on Mac), or right-click anywhere and choose Inspect.

3

Navigate to Storage → Cookies

In the developer tools panel top bar, click the Application tab (if narrow, click >> to reveal it). On the left panel under Storage, expand Cookies and click https://my.uscis.gov.

4

Copy the _myuscis_session_rx Value

Look down the Name column for _myuscis_session_rx. Double-click its row under the Value column, copy it (Ctrl + C / Cmd + C), and paste it into the Session Cookie field here.

🔒 Privacy Note: The _myuscis_session_rx cookie is temporary and expires when your session ends. It does not store your account password and is never saved permanently by this app.
